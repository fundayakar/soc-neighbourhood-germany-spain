## Description

This deposit accompanies “Separating the contributions of neighbouring values and spatial arrangement to soil organic carbon prediction in Germany and Spain.” It includes the modelling and analysis code, unlabelled predictor arrays, frozen spatial partitions, and aggregate results for the separate German and Spanish cohorts. It covers the main CNN input comparisons, the Spanish training-set size and NDVI analyses, and the German predictor-year and GPS extraction-centre sensitivity analyses. Scripts regenerate the principal manuscript tables from the archived aggregates. Refitting requires independently licensed LUCAS 2018 SOC records. Individual SOC measurements, point-level predictions and fitted checkpoints are excluded.
